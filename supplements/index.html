Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to take everything the quiz recommends?

No. The quiz ranks products by how well they match your answers. Start with the top one or two and add more if you want. Each recommendation shows the reason it was chosen.

How does the subscription work?

You get 20% off and free shipping on every delivery. Choose 30, 60 or 90 day intervals. You can skip, swap products, pause or cancel any time from your account, and there's no minimum number of orders.

How are your products tested?

Each batch is tested by an independent, ISO 17025-accredited lab for identity, potency, microbes and heavy metals. Results are matched to the lot number printed on your bottle.

Can I take these with medication?

Some ingredients, like ashwagandha, vitamin K2 and caffeine, can interact with medications or health conditions. Each product lists its cautions. If you take prescription medication, check with your doctor first.

What if it's not for me?

Try anything for 30 days. If you're not happy, we'll refund your first order, even if the container is empty.
